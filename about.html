About Me

Family Man · Entrepreneur · Creator

I’m Colin Tull — a husband, father, and builder based in the Tampa Bay area. I work full-time in corporate America while running a small business, producing music, and spending a lot of time learning and tinkering with technology.

Family comes first. I’m married and raising a young daughter, and the ordinary rhythms of that life shape how I approach everything else.

On the business side, I’m a small business owner focused on product development, sales, and operations. I also create and sell digital products and share content around the process of building things while balancing work and family.

Technology is a major thread. I spend a lot of time customizing and living in Linux environments — window managers, editors, scripting, servers, and small hardware projects. I’m drawn to tools that reward curiosity and give me more control over how things work, whether that’s refining a desktop setup, writing scripts, or repurposing old hardware.

I also produce progressive house music and use writing, photos, and video as a way to document family life, projects, fitness, and the ongoing work of trying to stay consistent. This site is a place for the things I’m building, creating, and exploring.
