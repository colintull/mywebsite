# I think tech is my thing.

I have friends with some very interesting hobbies. I have a friend who is a great wood worker and leather worker. I have a friend who restores pinball machines. One of my friends has a huge arsenal of guns and knows everything about them. I have friends who are great cooks, who make their own mead. So many people I know have their thing.

I have had multiple things throughout my life. I loved sports growing up. [Music](..music.html) has been and still is something I love doing. I've tried things like gardening and cooking and acting. 

I always come back to computers.

Maybe it is because I grew up in the late 90s and early 2000s when computers started really becoming part of our daily lives. I've just always been fascinated by them. I remember thinking, while watching my friends brick their computers, how do they make Limewire do all of this? How do people make something like that? Learning HTML and CSS made me feel like a wizard. Making my first website for my audio design company was exhilirating. I remember just staring at all the lines of HTML and being so proud of what I accomplished.

Over the years I have dipped my toes into computers, watching videos on programming languages like C and Python. Going through Network Chuck's Windows Fundamentals course. Downloading linux was monumental for me. I'll never forget booting into ubuntu and opening a terminal just to type ls. I felt like an engineer. I actually kick myself when I think about that time circa 2016. I remember thinking maybe I should focus on learning how to type properly before diving into this computer thing, and then didn't. I always tell myself if I coud go back and start college over I would have studied computers from day 1, as well as attend class. I feel like a poster child of having no idea what I wanted to do with my life after high school but I know that isn't something unique about me. Luckily though, I live in an age where I can learn anything and everything for free and I plan on it.

I built this website in a weekend by hand. (Admittedly I used some help from Grok to help me understand why things certain things work the way they do and how I can get my website to behave certain ways, but I think that is a topic for a later post.) I have always wanted my own personal website, I just never really knew what I would use it for. Thanks to a $6 domain name and free hosting from GitHub though I have it, and the more I have thought about it, I know how I want to use it.

I want to document my journey to learning computers and everything related to them. I have many skills to learn and many projects to get started on but it is incredibly exciting.

Thanks for visiting this page and taking the time to read this.

This is going to be a long journey. Maybe some day soon my friends will say "My friend Colin, he's really great with tech and computers. That's his thing."
